About Cactus Watch

Home/About

Cactus Watch is a mobile application designed to provide convenient access to public incident information through a simple and easy-to-use experience. The app was created to make public information more accessible through modern mapping, notifications, and enhanced viewing features.

Cactus Watch is intended solely for informational and situational awareness purposes. It is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency, fire department, EMS agency, public safety agency, or emergency communications center.

Information displayed within the app may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable, and not all incidents may be displayed. Cactus Watch is not an emergency notification service and should not be relied upon for emergency response or life-safety decisions.

Data Source

Incident information is sourced from the City of Phoenix Fire Department's publicly available active-incidents data feed:
https://maps.phoenix.gov/phxfire/rest/services/Active_Incidents__Public/MapServer

Cactus Watch does not represent a government entity and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of the City of Phoenix, any fire department, law enforcement agency, or emergency-services provider.
