Cactus Watch

Designed for
Situational Awareness

Cactus Watch is a mobile application designed to provide convenient access to public incident information through an intuitive and easy to use experience. Cactus Watch Plus includes an interactive detailed map, day, night, and satellite map views, custom color overlays, push notifications, manual refresh, and enhanced incident views.

Now available on iPhone and iPad — coming soon to Google Play. Questions? Email support@cactuswatch.com.

Cactus Watch is provided solely for informational and situational awareness purposes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency, fire department, EMS agency, public safety agency, or emergency communications center. Incident information may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable. Not all incidents are displayed.

Cactus Watch is not an emergency notification service. Do not rely on Cactus Watch for life-safety decisions. In an emergency, call 911.
