Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy

Effective 3 June 2026.

Overview. Cactus Watch is designed to minimize data collection. We do not display advertisements or use advertising networks. We do not use cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

We do not require or collect user-provided personal data such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Payment information for subscriptions is processed by Apple or Google and is not provided to us.

We process only limited technical information necessary to operate the app, provide features you choose to use, and maintain system stability and security.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process limited technical information, including:

  • Device and application identifiers used to operate and maintain the app
  • Push notification tokens used to deliver notifications you enable
  • Subscription status information used to provide paid features
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information used to maintain and improve stability
  • Basic technical connection information, such as IP address, used for security, abuse prevention, and operational purposes

How We Use Information

We use limited technical information to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Provide features and services you choose to use
  • Deliver notifications
  • Manage subscriptions and access to paid features
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or technical issues

Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to support the operation of the app, including services related to hosting, infrastructure, data storage, analytics, crash reporting, push notifications, and subscription management.

These providers may process limited technical information on our behalf to help operate and maintain the app. Their processing of information is subject to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual obligations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited technical information with service providers as described in this policy, or if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and services.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect the data we process. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain technical information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will provide notice within the app or by reasonable means. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support@cactuswatch.com.
