Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy

Effective 30 May 2026. Plain English, no legalese.

Short version. We do not ask for your name or email, we do not sell your data, and we do not show ads. We do collect a few technical identifiers needed to run the app, deliver the alerts you turn on, and keep it stable. The details are below.

What we collect

Data Why Stored
A device identifier (Apple identifierForVendor) and a random app client ID Tell devices apart so we can rate-limit fairly and tie your saved alert choices to your install On your device and in our database while the app is installed
Push notification token Deliver the station alerts you choose to turn on (Cactus Watch Plus) In our database until you disable alerts or uninstall
Your saved stations/units and which incidents you have already been alerted about So alerts go to the right device and you are not pinged twice for the same call In our database while the app is installed
Subscription status from Apple (via RevenueCat): a subscriber ID and paid/not-paid Unlock Cactus Watch Plus features With RevenueCat and in our records while you subscribe
Crash and diagnostic reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Find and fix crashes With Google Firebase per their retention
Approximate location, only the moment you tap the locate-me button on the map Center the map on you Not stored on our servers and never shared
Your IP address and the fact that a request hit our API Rate-limit abusive clients and aggregate capacity counters In server logs for 7 days, then rotated

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or mailing address
  • Background or continuous location (location is used only on the locate-me tap, never in the background)
  • Your contacts, photos, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data — we do not track you across other apps or websites
  • Your payment card details — Apple handles billing; we never see your card

What you see in the app

Active fire and EMS incidents from the City of Phoenix Fire Department public incident dashboard. Each row is what dispatch publishes: a location, a category, and a timestamp, with responding-unit and dispatch-channel detail available on Cactus Watch Plus. We add a human label for cryptic codes (for example, Phoenix 962 becomes Vehicle Crash in our display) but the underlying data is theirs, not ours.

The paid tier

If you subscribe to Cactus Watch Plus, Apple's App Store (through RevenueCat) handles your card details. We never see them. We receive only a subscriber identifier and a paid/not-paid status. To deliver the push alerts you turn on, we ask for notification permission at that moment, and you can revoke it in iOS Settings any time.

Third parties

We use Google Firebase for crash reporting (Crashlytics), push-notification delivery (Cloud Messaging), and storing your saved alert preferences (Firestore); RevenueCat for subscription management; Mapbox for the map tiles shown on the Plus map; and DigitalOcean (servers) and Cloudflare (DNS/CDN) for hosting. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel, Segment, or any cross-app ad-tracking SDK, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

Data we attribute

Phoenix Fire incident data comes from the City of Phoenix Fire Department public ArcGIS endpoint, used under the City Open Data Policy. We poll once per minute, cache it for our users, and add attribution in the app. We are not affiliated with the City of Phoenix.

Children

Cactus Watch is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app shows public safety incidents, which can include sensitive content; parental discretion is advised.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what we collect, we will update this page and bump the Effective date above. For material changes that affect existing users (extremely unlikely given our data minimization), we will surface an in-app notice.

Contact

Questions, complaints, or data deletion requests: support@cactuswatch.com. We respond within 7 days.
